# MANDI MITHRA — Role-Based Operational User Guide

---

### 1. Farmer Operational Guide
1. **Login**: Access `http://localhost:5173/login`, choose Farmer login, and enter your registered mobile number. In development mode, use standard OTP `123456`.
2. **Register Farm Land**: Navigate to **My Farms** $\to$ **Register Farm**, provide survey number, acreage, and crops cultivated.
3. **Book Slot**: Select your nearby Mandi Collection Centre and book an appointment for your estimated harvest weight.
4. **Track Delivery & Grade**: On the day of delivery, present your digital QR token at the gate. Review the inspector's Grade (A/B/C) and Quality Score in real time on your dashboard.
5. **View Settlements**: Inspect your digital payout voucher under **Settlements**. View gross amount, transparent mandi fees, and bank transfer timestamp.

---

### 2. Quality Inspector Operational Guide
1. **Intake Queue**: Open the Quality Inspector portal to view lots queued for evaluation.
2. **Conduct Test**: Enter sample weight, moisture %, foreign matter %, and defective percentage.
3. **AI Assistance**: Review AI Assistant observations for elevated moisture warnings ($>14\%$).
4. **Submit Grade**: Confirm final Grade and accept or partially reject the lot.

---

### 3. Collection Centre Manager Guide
1. **Gate Operations**: Monitor daily intake queues, token arrivals, and active weighbridges.
2. **Storage Routing**: Ensure accepted produce is immediately assigned to warehouse bays.
3. **Settlement Approval**: Review pending farmer vouchers and approve disbursement.
4. **Dispute Redressal**: Mediate any weight or grading grievances raised by farmers.

---

### 4. B2B Buyer Guide
1. **Marketplace Catalog**: Browse real-time verified inventories categorized by crop, moisture grade, and warehouse location.
2. **Create Purchase Order**: Place bulk orders specifying delivery deadlines and destination address.
3. **Track Order & Shipment**: Track order approval, lot allocation, and live truck dispatch.
4. **Delivery Receipt**: Confirm receipt of goods, log destination gross weight, and sign the digital delivery receipt.

---

### 5. Logistics Coordinator Guide
1. **Fleet Management**: Register transport trucks, payload capacities, and assign qualified drivers.
2. **Dispatch Manifest**: Create shipments for approved purchase orders, verify loaded weight against vehicle capacity, and generate departure manifest.
3. **In-Transit Risk**: Monitor delayed shipments flagged by the automated transit risk detector.

---

### 6. Super Admin & District Officer Guide
1. **Command Center**: Monitor district-wide procurement volumes, total tonnage, and warehouse utilization %.
2. **AI & Anomaly Alerts**: View real-time anomaly alerts (near-capacity warehouses, delayed trucks, elevated rejections).
3. **Global Search & Export**: Use `⌘K` to look up any entity across the network and export auditable CSV spreadsheets.
